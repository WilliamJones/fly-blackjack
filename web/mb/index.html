The mechanism

Kenyon cells encode odour identity sparsely — about 6% fire for any one smell. When dopamine arrives at the same moment, the synapse from those cells onto the output neuron is depressed. Punishing an odour does not make an avoidance signal shout; it makes the approach signal go quiet. Which output neurons a dopamine cell can reach is fixed by measured anatomy, not by a parameter.

Measured vs modelled

Measured: 682 projection neurons, 1,200 Kenyon cells, 97 output neurons, 332 dopaminergic neurons, every connection between them, and the soma coordinates you are looking at — MaleCNS v1.0, CC BY 4.0. Modelled: rate units rather than spikes, the learning rate and decay, the sparsity imposed by winner-take-all instead of APL feedback, and which glomeruli each odour drives.
